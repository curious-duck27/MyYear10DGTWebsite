To play, use arrow keys and the space bar.

Here is my platform game that I made on GDevelop. I learnt and played around with different types of games and new techniques. I found this topic enjoyable because I liked playing around with tricks and ways to create this game. It took me a while to design as the pixel art took longer than I had imagined. After thinking my game was too basic, I went ahead and created new platform tiles that look similar to the normal ones. These new tiles killed the character each time they came in contact. I think this adds a fun aspect because it creates some obstacles.
